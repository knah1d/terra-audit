Introduction & Background

Flooded rice paddies are a major source of agricultural methane: waterlogged soil turns anaerobic and favours methane-producing microbes. Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) lets the water drop below the soil surface before re-irrigating, cutting methane while maintaining yield. Free Sentinel-1 radar sees through cloud and shows when a field is flooded.

Problem Statement

Practice claims are hard to verify

Irrigation logs are incomplete; sensors on every smallholder plot are too costly.

Evidence gets separated from the number

Spreadsheets lose the factors, methodology version and history behind a result.

Verification needs traceability

Verifiers must trace every tonne of CO₂e back to its evidence and rule.

Gap: remote-sensing tools and carbon spreadsheets live apart; reviewers reconstruct by hand which plots, logs and methodology version produced a figure.

Research Objectives

Detect AWD from space

Flooding & drydown from free Sentinel-1 SAR, labelled as estimates.

Scoped carbon engines

VM0051 v1.1 (rice, QA3) and VM0042 v2.2 (cropland ALM) with visible intermediates.

Cropland evidence

Practices, production, soil sampling, lab, custody & livestock records.

Readiness & leakage

Missing or unsupported rules shown, never silently counted as met or zero.

Frozen, versioned results

Every calculation freezes its evidence, inputs and results.

Internal review

A second person approves before a result reaches a report.

AI as an aid only

ML detectors & LLM explanations never change a calculated value.

Multi-user platform

Org & project access control, background jobs, history & export.

Discussion

Free radar can surface AWD. Sentinel-1 drydown signatures were detected without field sensors, but they remain satellite estimates: a field can drain and re-flood between two passes.

Reproducibility comes from freezing. Calculating only from an immutable snapshot means a report rebuilt later shows the same numbers and evidence (TC-04).

Failing closed beats guessing. Gates block oversized projects, unsupported rules and incomplete leakage instead of reporting zero. Golden-value tests caught a wrong emission factor (1.4 → 0.85).

ML is a second opinion, not proof. About 71% of unseen plots are classified correctly with out-of-region labels, so the model stays advisory.

10

Conclusion

Terra-Audit shows that the evidence behind an agricultural carbon claim can be collected, calculated under VM0051 and VM0042, and reviewed in one system, with every result traceable from field evidence to the final estimate and the calculation kept separate from the AI around it. A result the implemented methodology cannot support is blocked, not reported.
